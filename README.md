# MEDICAL-BILLING-IMPORTANT-TERM-BASIC-LEARNING
MEDICAL BILLING IMPORTANT TERM BASIC LEARNING 

**1. Payment Posting Terms**

| Term | Simple Defination | 
|------|-------------------|
| Billed Amount | Total money the hospital or doctor asks for a service. | 
| Allowed Amount | Maximum amount the insurance company accepts for a covered service under the plan. | 
| Insurance Payment|	Money paid by the insurance company to the healthcare provider. | 
 | Deductible |	Money the patient must pay before insurance starts paying for eligible services under the plan. | 
 | Coinsurance |	A percentage of the medical bill the patient must pay. |
 | Copayment (Copay) |	A fixed amount the patient pays for a medical service. |
 | Contractual Adjustment |	Money the provider agrees to remove from the bill because of its insurance contract. |
| Patient Responsibility |	The amount the patient has to pay. |
| Write-off |	An amount removed from the account that the provider will not collect. |
| Outstanding Balance | 	Money that is still unpaid. | 
| Payment Posting | 	Recording insurance or patient payments in the patient's account. | 

**2. Insurance and Claim Terms**
|          Term           |             Simple Defination                       | 
|-------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| Claim | 	A request sent to insurance asking for payment for medical services.|
 | Claim Submission |	Sending a medical bill to the insurance company.| 
| Claim Processing | 	Insurance checking the claim and deciding how much to pay. |
| Claim Approval |	Insurance agrees to pay the eligible amount.|
| Claim Denial |	Insurance refuses to pay a claim or a service. |
 | Claim Rejection |	Insurance or the clearinghouse does not accept a claim for processing, often due to errors.| 
| Resubmission | 	Sending a corrected or previously rejected claim again.|
| Prior Authorization | 	Permission obtained from insurance before certain medical services.|
| Eligibility Verification | 	Checking whether a patient's insurance is active.|
| Benefits Verification |	Checking what medical services the insurance plan covers.|

**3. Important Medical Billing Documents**
|          Term           |             Simple Defination                       | 
|-------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| ERA | 	Electronic payment information sent by insurance to the healthcare provider.|
| EOB |	A statement that explains to the patient how insurance processed a claim. It is not a bill.|
| CMS-1500 |A form used to submit professional medical claims.|
| UB-04 |	A form used by hospitals and other institutional providers to submit claims.|
| Superbill |	A document listing the services, diagnosis codes and charges for a patient visit.|
| Medical Record |	A document containing the patient's medical history, diagnosis and treatment.|

**4. Denial Management Terms**

|          Term           |             Simple Defination                       | 
|-------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| Denial Management |	Finding why insurance did not pay and fixing the problem |
|AR (Accounts Receivable) |	Money that a healthcare provider is waiting to receive.|
|AR Follow-up |	Contacting insurance to check the status of unpaid claims.|
| Appeal |	Asking insurance to review a denied claim again.|
| Timely Filing |	The deadline for sending a claim to insurance.|
| Medical Necessity |	The treatment or service must be medically appropriate for the patient's condition.|
| Duplicate Claim |	Sending the same claim more than once.|
| CO-16 |	Claim has missing information or contains an error.|
| CO-18 |	Claim is identified as a duplicate.|
| CO-45 |	Charge is higher than the allowed amount; the difference is adjusted.|
| CO-197 |	Required authorization or notification is missing.|

**5. Important Medical Billing Departments**

|          Term           |             Simple Defination                       | 
|-------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| Front Office |	Staff who register patients and check insurance details.|
| Medical Coding |	Converting diagnoses and medical procedures into standard codes. |
| Medical Billing |	Preparing and sending claims to insurance companies.|
| Payment Posting |	Recording payments received from insurance and patients.|
| Denial Management |	Correcting problems with denied claims.|
| AR Department |	Following up on unpaid claims.|
| RCM |	Revenue Cycle Management – the complete process from patient registration to final payment.|

**6. Remember these five important terms**

Quick revision

1. Claim: Request money from insurance.

2. ERA: Payment details for the provider.

3. EOB: Claim explanation for the patient.

4. Denial: Insurance refuses payment.

5. AR: Money still waiting to be collected.
